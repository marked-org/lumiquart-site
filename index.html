Lumiquart

A puzzle game of light and quarter turns. Tap a lantern to turn its 2×2 square; line up three of a colour and they light up, each firing a beam the way it faces. One grid a day, the same for everyone: twenty moves, then share your score with friends.

iPhone first. No account, no ads, no tracking. In French and English.
