Lumiquart

Privacy

Lumiquart collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

What stays on your phone

  • The moves of today's grid and its thinking time, so you can leave and come back to the same game. Other days' moves and times are deleted.
  • Your settings: lantern style, music, sounds, vibrations, "Without time", "Extended time", the last mode you played, and whether you have seen the lessons and the "Without time" offer.
  • For each daily grid you finish: its date, your score and your best chain (the last 400 days), to show your days in a row, grids played and best score.
  • Your Endless game: its board number, its moves and your best Endless score.
  • Your Time Trial: today's run in progress and your best score.
  • Your Challenges progress: the stars you earned on each level.

Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app never does

No account, no server, no internet connection, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code. The daily grid is computed on your phone from the date.

Sharing

The Share button opens your phone's share sheet with a short text (grid number, score, best chain, time unless "Without time" is on, and a few coloured squares). You choose whether and where to send it; the app sends nothing itself.

Test versions

If you test Lumiquart through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and the feedback you send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.
