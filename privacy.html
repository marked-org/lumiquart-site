Lumiquart

Privacy

Lumiquart collects no data. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

What stays on your phone

  • The moves of today's grid, so you can leave and come back to the same game. Other days' moves are deleted.
  • The lantern style you chose in Settings, and whether you have seen "How to play".
  • For each daily grid you finish: its date, your score and your best chain (the last 400 days), to show your days in a row, grids played and best score.
  • Your Veillée (endless) game: its board number, its moves and your best Veillée score.
  • Your Chemin (Path) progress: the stars you earned on each level.

Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app never does

No account, no server, no internet connection, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code. The daily grid is computed on your phone from the date.

Sharing

The Share button opens your phone's share sheet with a short text (grid number, score, best chain and a few coloured squares). You choose whether and where to send it; the app sends nothing itself.

Test versions

If you test Lumiquart through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and the feedback you send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.
